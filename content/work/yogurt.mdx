## Brief

YoGurt is a digital storefront designed for an artisan Greek yoghurt brand based in Jakarta. While the brand enjoys a dedicated local following for its dense protein macros and daily fresh batches, its primary sales channel previously relied entirely on manual WhatsApp messages and bank transfer slips.

The core design mandate was clear: build a modern, appetite-inducing ecommerce experience that removes friction from reordering, showcases fresh batches transparently, and preserves direct conversational customer care.

The resulting interface balances a warm, approachable brand voice with rapid single-page conversion paths, immediate quantity adjustments, and dynamic free shipping thresholds.

## Approach

The visual direction centers on natural, dairy-inspired tones: a warm cream canvas complemented by honey-ochre action buttons and deep olive forest greens for nutritional credibility.

To bridge health consciousness with everyday joy, the interface integrates approachable copy, micro-nutrition badges (High Protein, Probiotic, Low Fat), and gamified educational trivia cards alongside standard ecommerce primitives.

## Engineering

The application is built on Next.js with React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Key engineering priorities focused on immediate feedback, persistent shopping state, and zero-distraction checkout flows.

A persistent slide-out cart drawer slides over the active viewport without forcing full-page reloads. It features an animated progress bar computing the remaining spend needed to trigger free delivery (Rp 150.000), motivating users to add an extra jar before proceeding.

<ScreenPair leftSrc="/work/yogurt/09-cart.png" leftTitle="Slide-Over Cart & Free Delivery Meter" leftTag="Cart Architecture" leftCaption="Dynamic slide-out drawer providing instant feedback, item quantity steppers, and free-shipping progress calculation." rightSrc="/work/yogurt/10-checkout.png" rightTitle="Frictionless Single-Page Checkout" rightTag="Checkout Flow" rightCaption="Two-column order layout separating recipient credentials and Jakarta delivery notes from a sticky order summary." />

Recognizing local purchasing habits, the platform also integrates a direct WhatsApp ordering gateway on both the conversion banner and dedicated contact view, pre-filling customer cart selections directly into a WhatsApp draft for custom batch or corporate catering inquiries.
